About

I work on generative AI, from model architectures to post-training.

My recent work studies how a diffusion transformer can reuse its parameters and spend its compute where the noise level calls for it. I am also interested in large language and vision-language models, and in reinforcement learning as a way to post-train generative models.

Publications

DyLoMo-DiT: Dynamic Looped Mixture-of-Experts Diffusion Transformers

Lernie Li, Yuanliu Liu, Pengfei Xiong, Wei Zhang

arXiv:2611.30844, 2026

DyLoMo-DiT reuses parameters instead of adding them: a weight-shared loop applies the middle layers several times, sparse experts decide which parameters each token uses, and the number of passes changes with the noise level. At the parameter and compute budget of DiT, it lowers FID on ImageNet 256×256 by 11–24%.
